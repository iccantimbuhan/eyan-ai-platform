# Eyan AI Platform

Version: 1.0

---

# Vision

Eyan AI Platform is a self-hosted AI workspace designed for developers, AI engineers, businesses, and technical virtual assistants.

The platform provides a single interface to interact with multiple AI providers, manage prompts, organize projects, generate content, and build AI-powered workflows.

Unlike cloud-only solutions, Eyan AI Platform gives users complete ownership of their data while remaining provider-agnostic and extensible.

The long-term vision is to become a complete AI Operating System similar to ChatGPT Team, Claude Team, and Open WebUI, but optimized for self-hosting, extensibility, and enterprise readiness.

---

# Mission

Build a modern AI platform that enables anyone to:

- Connect multiple AI providers
- Build AI-powered projects
