**Restaurant Operations Platform — Cash Reconciliation — Amendment: Cash/Electronic Discount Split — COMPLETE.** The manager reported a second, distinct problem beyond the POS-scoping amendment below: their POS can't record a discount against the actual amount received at all, so staff enter one combined `discountsTotal` figure covering both a cash-reducing portion and an electronic/card-reducing portion together (e.g. €61.35 = €49.45 cash + €11.90 electronic), and the formula was subtracting the *entire* combined figure from physical cash — overstating the shortfall by the electronic portion every time. This is explicitly not a platform-wide rule (most restaurants never touch it); the architecture stays generic and restaurant-configurable, driven entirely by data, never by hardcoded payment-method or POS-source names. One additional additive, nullable column, `DailySalesRecord.cashDiscountTotal` — `null` means "not configured," reproducing the pre-amendment formula (the entire `discountsTotal` reduces cash) byte-for-byte for every existing record, with zero backfill and zero invented allocation. `computeCashReconciliation()` now derives `cashReducingDiscount = cashDiscountTotal ?? discountsTotal` and uses that (never the raw `discountsTotal`) everywhere cash is reduced — composing unchanged with the existing POS-scoping rule from the first amendment. `electronicDiscountTotal` (`discountsTotal - cashDiscountTotal`) is computed only for display, next to the restaurant's Card/Electronic payment methods, never itself subtracted from cash and never a second stored source of truth. Also fixed, discovered during inspection: `SalesAggregationService`'s weekly `totalExpectedCash` was re-derived from `totalPhysicalCashBasis - discountsTotal` (a shortcut that happened to be correct before this amendment) rather than accumulated from each day's own computed `expectedCash` — would have silently ignored the electronic-discount portion at the weekly level too; now accumulates per-day, keeping daily and weekly permanently in sync. `DailySalesDialog` gained an optional "Cash Discount (optional)" field next to "Manual Discounts Today," explicitly labeled as opt-in and restaurant-specific; `CashReconciliationCard` now shows "Cash Discount Applied" (not the full discount) under the discounted POS bucket, and a new "Electronic Discount" line next to Card/Electronic payment methods. See ADR-0043's second amendment section.

**Amendment, same sprint — POS-scoped manual discount.** The manager reported the original formula was wrong for a real multi-POS case: it summed physical cash across every POS source into one pool and subtracted the whole day's discount from that combined pool, so a discount meant only for POS 1 (e.g. Trust Pay/Card Payment + Cash Draw) mathematically bled into POS 2's untouched cash (e.g. Bolt Cash). Per the user's explicit "do not code yet" instruction, ran an architecture-investigation-only pass first, confirming the exact gap and proposing the smallest fix before implementation. One additional additive column, `DailySalesRecord.discountPosSourceId` (nullable FK → `PosSource`, `onDelete: SetNull`) — `null` means "all POS sources," reproducing the pre-amendment combined-total formula byte-for-byte for every existing record (proved, not just asserted: the overall `expectedCash` formula is mathematically `physicalCashBasis - discountsTotal` regardless of scope, since summing gross across every bucket and subtracting one flat discount is scope-invariant — only the new `cashByPosSource[]` per-bucket *display* breakdown changes with the scope, never the total). `computeCashReconciliation()` now groups cash entries into per-POS-source buckets (reusing the same `"unassigned"`-bucket convention `SalesAggregationService` already used elsewhere) and applies the discount to exactly the bucket matching `discountPosSourceId`, leaving every other bucket's expected cash equal to its own gross cash. `CashReconciliationCard` was redesigned to show "Discountable Physical Cash — POS 1" and "Non-Discountable Physical Cash — POS 2" as visually separate sections (falling back to a neutral, undifferentiated layout when no POS scope is set), and `DailySalesDialog` gained a "Discount applies to" select sourced dynamically from the restaurant's own `PosSource` catalog — never hardcoded POS names. See ADR-0043, including its amendment section.
